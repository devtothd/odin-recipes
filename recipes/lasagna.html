Lasagna

Description

The recipe contains meat, sauce, cheese and everything delicious!

Ingedients

  • Meat
  • Cheese
  • Tomato
  • White sauce
  • Seasonings

Steps

  1. Smear
  2. Top with sauces
  3. Lay out lasagna sheets, then top with more sauce
  4. Repeat
  5. Cover with lasagna sheets, pour remaining sauce then sprinkle with cheese
